## Direction problem — likely causes, most likely first

1. **Wrong geometry used for rotation compensation.** The camera image turns
   with the forearm. The tracker computes the forearm angle from MPos using
   `ARM_L1/L2` and `ELBOW_SIGN`, and corrects J by (φ − φ0). If the lengths or
   elbow sign are wrong, or the camera does not actually rotate that way, the
   correction adds error. It is right at the calibration pose and gets worse
   the further the arm moves from it.
   *Symptom:* centring works near the calibration pose and curves or diverges
   elsewhere.
2. **Kinematics mismatch.** If FluidNC drives the joints as rotary axes
   (jogs in degrees), then `JOG_CARTESIAN 1` is wrong and J is only valid
   locally. `fluidnc_check.py` answers this in one run.
3. **Noisy Jacobian.** FOMO puts the target on a 12×12 grid (one cell ≈ 0.17
   in image units). If calibration shifts were small (< ~0.15), J's direction
   can be off by tens of degrees.
   *Symptom:* wrong direction even right next to the calibration pose.
4. **Gain or latency.** With `kp` too high relative to the loop delay (frame +
   `horizon_s` ≈ 0.4 s), the arm overshoots and oscillates. That looks like
   direction trouble but isn't.
5. **Target moved during calibration**, or arm backlash.

## Plan for tomorrow

1. Run `tools/fluidnc_check.py <fluidnc-ip>` (read-only). Fix `JOG_CARTESIAN`,
   `ARM_L1/L2`, `ELBOW_SIGN` from its output. Set the real `LIM_*`.
2. Calibrate with the head still, near the middle of the work area. Save the
   `cal_log` and `J` from `/status`. Both J columns should have a shift of at
   least ~0.15.
3. **Isolate the cause with gentle settings:** `kp 0.3`, `vmax 20`.
   - Put the head slightly off-centre **at the calibration pose**. Does the
     arm go straight to it?
     - No → J is wrong (cause 3 or 5): recalibrate with bigger steps.
     - Yes → go on.
   - Repeat far from the calibration pose.
     - Fails only there → rotation compensation (cause 1). Test with
       `CAMERA_ROTATES_WITH_FOREARM 0`.
4. Once the direction is right, raise `kp` and `vmax` gradually.
